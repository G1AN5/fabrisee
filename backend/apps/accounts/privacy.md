# Privacy notice

FabriSee is the project coordination system of {company_name}. In line with the Data Privacy
Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), this notice explains what personal data FabriSee keeps
about you and why.

## What FabriSee keeps

- Your name, work email address, phone number, job title, and the company you belong to.
- What you do in FabriSee: the requests, comments, decisions, and files you submit, each with
  its date and time.
- The date and time of your last sign-in.

FabriSee does not ask for birthdays, government IDs, or payment details.

## Why it is kept

- To coordinate display projects between {company_name} and its clients: requests, quotations,
  design approvals, and progress updates.
- To keep a reliable record of who decided what and when.
- To send you notifications and reminders about your projects.

## Who can see it

- Staff of {company_name} who work on your projects.
- Users of your own company who share your projects.
- Other clients never see your data or your projects.

## Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, or remove your personal data. Contact your project manager or the
FabriSee administrator at {company_name}.
